What Rupert does

Four jobs, one workspace.

Most shops juggle a register app, a stock spreadsheet, a timesheet tool and a bookkeeper's inbox. Rupert puts them in one place, so each sale moves the stock, the drawer and the books together.

Sell

A register built for the counter.

Ring up sales full screen on a tablet or laptop. Search or scan, take cash or card, add a tip, and the sale lands in the same list as your online orders.

  • Cash and card tenders, tips and receipts
  • Registers, customers and every channel's sales
  • Online and in-store orders side by side

Stock

One count of what you have, in every store.

See every item by location in a sortable grid. Count, transfer and reorder from the same place, and catch low stock before the shelf is empty.

  • Inventory by location, with how fresh each count is
  • Physical counts, transfers and stock alerts
  • Purchase orders, vendors and bulk recipes

Books

Know where the money went.

Cash flow shows what came into the bank, what went out and what was sold, side by side. Day close and the ledger keep the books in step with the register.

  • Cash flow, statements, sales tax and forecasts
  • Day close and daily reconciliation
  • General ledger, expenses and bank reconciliation

Team

Who is working, and what they are owed.

Staff clock in at the register or on their phone. Schedules, timesheets and hours by store come together, ready to review and pay.

  • Time clock, timesheets and shifts
  • Leave, PTO and pay runs
  • Team chat and training progress

How a day runs

From opening the drawer to closing the books.

  1. Open

    Start the register.

    Count the drawer and open a register for the shift.

  2. Sell

    Ring it up.

    Sales at the counter and from connected stores land in one list.

  3. Stock

    Stock follows.

    Each sale moves the count. Low items show up before the shelf is empty.

  4. Close

    Close the day.

    Rupert checks the register against cash and card deposits and flags what does not match.

  5. Books

    Hand off clean books.

    Closed days post to the ledger for your bookkeeper to review and approve.

Questions

Before you get started.

Do I need a card to sign up?

No. Create your account with a verified Google identity. Paid checkout is a separate step inside your workspace.

How does the referral discount work?

An active paying Rupert customer can share their referral code. Eligible new subscriptions receive 50% off the $499 monthly price for 12 months in exchange for a 12-month commitment. You pay $249.50 per month, then $499 per month after the first year. Taxes may apply.

Who bills my subscription?

Stripe processes subscription payments using the CSP Cyber Security Pilot billing account. You review the amount before confirming payment.

Does Google sign-in connect all my business accounts?

No. It verifies your identity for Rupert. Each supported provider connection needs its own authorization and setup.
