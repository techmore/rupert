ERP modules

Every module registers in app/models/module_registry.rb, which drives the nav and the permission gate. Modules can be shipped, hidden, or permission-limited per role.

Overview

Dashboard

Per-user customizable widget layout: key sales stats, per-channel revenue, attention items, stock alerts, and sync / reconcile / goal history. Widgets can be reordered and toggled.

Commerce

Sales

Daily sales journal in spreadsheet style — an hourly × location pivot plus every sale of the day in arrival order, with a tracking indicator per order.

Registers

POS cash-drawer sessions: open a register, see sales per tender, and settle with a physical cash count at end of day.

Customers

Unified CRM across channels with Ransack search, pagination, and lifetime value. Add, edit, and view each customer's order history.

Inventory

Products and variants mirrored from Shopify with quantities on both sides, drift vs Square, and SKU linkage status.

Inventory counts

Manual count sheets with a prepared-blank workflow, then submit / approve / reject / reopen.

Orders

Detail page per order with a printable invoice and packing slip, shipping address, line items, payments, and fulfillment tracking.

Operations

Reports

Sales, financial (P&L), inventory, and operations reports with 7/30/90/365-day windows and CSV export.

Reconcile

SKU-level drift between Shopify and Square with a generated adjustment plan and per-SKU priority policy.

Ledger

Central transaction stream across both platforms, filterable by window and source.

Projects

Simple project + task tracking with status transitions (start, hold, complete, archive).

Goals & KPIs

Goals with activate / achieve / abandon, each with key performance indicators and reading history.

Team

Employees

Directory of who can sign in, with role assignment, activate / deactivate, and removal.

Permissions

A role × permission matrix (23 permissions) with save and reset-to-defaults, plus per-person overrides on each employee's edit page.

System

Alerts

Low-stock and attention flags that can be resolved or ignored per row.

Sync

Run full, Shopify-only, or Square-only syncs; review run history and the log tail.

System health

Server load, memory, swap, disk, database, and slow-query monitoring.

Settings

Env import/export, database backup/restore, Google Drive backup, the Buzz agent, and office feature flags.

Warehouse

Secret vendor links with price multipliers and bulk tiers, plus a public token-gated storefront for each vendor.
