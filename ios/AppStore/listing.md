**Description [4000]**

```
Rupert is the back office for independent shops. It brings your sales, inventory, people and bookkeeping into one place, and this app puts it in your pocket.

TODAY AT A GLANCE
• Your start page shows today's sales, what needs attention and what your team is working on.
• Notifications tell you when a count, an order or a teammate needs you.

COUNT STOCK WITH YOUR CAMERA
• Open an inventory count and scan barcodes with the iPhone camera. Each scan saves straight to the count.
• Works alongside USB and Bluetooth scanners at the counter.

TEAM CHAT AND ASK RUPERT
• Talk with your team in Team chat, with photos and files.
• Ask Rupert questions about your business in plain words: "How did we do last Saturday?" or "What's running low?"

TIME, SCHEDULE AND PAY FOR YOUR TEAM
• Clock in and out in one tap, with breaks and meals; a PIN clock on the shop's register.
• Your shifts, open shifts to pick up, swaps and give-aways with your manager's approval.
• Time off requests and balances, and push notifications when your schedule is posted or a request is decided.
• Your pay at a glance: hours, estimated gross, card and cash tips, next payday.
• Managers approve corrections, time off, trades and timesheets from the phone.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
• Sales and customers from the systems your shop already uses, such as Shopify and Square.
• Inventory, purchasing and counts across locations.
• Staff schedules, time clock and timesheets.
• Bookkeeping, cash flow and reports.

BUILT FOR THE SHOP FLOOR
• Lock the app with Face ID, so a shared phone does not show your numbers.
• Home Screen quick actions jump straight to Clock in, Team chat, Ask Rupert or Notifications.
• Follows your workspace's colours and appearance.

Rupert is for businesses that use Rupert. Sign in with the account your business gave you, with Google or with email and password.
```
